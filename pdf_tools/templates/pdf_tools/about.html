About Pdfino

Pdfino is a free, browser-based PDF toolkit. Merge, split, compress, convert and edit your PDF files without installing any software. Every tool performs genuine document processing on the server - nothing is faked, and nothing is left half-finished.

We built Pdfino around three principles: files are handled securely and deleted automatically, every tool actually does what it says, and the interface works just as well on a phone as it does on a desktop.
