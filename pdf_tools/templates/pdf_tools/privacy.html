Privacy Policy

Files you upload

Files you upload are used only to perform the operation you requested. Generated files are automatically deleted from our servers after a short retention window (see the site footer for the current value). We do not read, share, sell, or use the content of your files for any other purpose.

What we store

We keep a small metadata record of each processing job (which tool was used, file size, and timestamps) so we can operate the service and clean up files reliably. We never store the contents of your documents in our database.

Accounts

Creating an account is optional. If you do, we store your username, email and a securely hashed password using Django's built-in authentication system.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be directed to the site administrator.
